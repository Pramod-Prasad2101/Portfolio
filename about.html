That curiosity has followed me into my work. It gives me the chance to look at information from different angles, to question assumptions, and to find the story that isn't always obvious. What drives me is the bigger picture, the connections, the patterns, and the small changes that can make a big difference. I enjoy looking at the bigger picture, seeing how things connect, and finding simple ways to make a difference. It's not about being right, it's about being useful.
